Local Attractions

Explore the ancient city of Byblos and Lebanon

Discover Byblos & Lebanon

IC2AI 2027 takes place in Byblos, one of the Mediterranean’s most historic destinations. Discover nearby coastal towns, cultural landmarks, and memorable day trips across Lebanon.

Byblos Archaeological Site

Explore the UNESCO-listed archaeological site of Byblos (Jbeil), with its Phoenician remains, ancient temples, and Crusader Castle. The historic site is a short drive from LAU Byblos Campus.

View on map

Old Souk & Waterfront

Stroll through the old souk, discover artisan shops and stone-paved lanes, and enjoy the picturesque harbor and waterfront restaurants—an inviting option after conference sessions.

View on map

Batroun – Historic Coastal City

Discover Batroun’s old harbor, traditional souk, Phoenician sea wall, coastal cafés, and famous fresh lemonade. This charming seaside city lies north of Byblos.

View on map

Anfeh – Coastal Village

Enjoy Anfeh’s turquoise coastline, blue-and-white waterfront at Tahet El Rih, and historic salt pans. Its relaxed seaside atmosphere makes it a distinctive coastal excursion.

View on map

Jeita Grotto

Visit Jeita Grotto’s remarkable limestone chambers and formations, south of Byblos. Access to individual cave sections and boat rides depends on the attraction’s operating conditions.

View on map

Harissa – Our Lady of Lebanon

Take in panoramic views of Jounieh Bay and the Mediterranean from the hilltop shrine at Harissa, one of Lebanon’s best-known landmarks.

View on map

Beirut City

Explore Lebanon’s capital, from the Corniche and National Museum to lively neighborhoods such as Gemmayzeh and Mar Mikhael. Travel time from Byblos varies with traffic.

View on map

Cedars of God

Discover the ancient cedar forest near Bsharri, part of a UNESCO World Heritage property. Mountain scenery and cooler temperatures make it a memorable longer excursion.

View on map

Baalbek Temples

Experience the monumental Roman temple complex at Baalbek, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in the Beqaa Valley. Plan this destination as a full-day excursion.

View on map
Travel tip: Attractions outside Byblos may require private transportation or a guided trip. Check opening hours, tickets, weather, and road conditions before traveling.

Practical Information
Currency: Lebanese Pound (LBP) & USD widely accepted
Language: Arabic (French & English widely spoken)
Time Zone: UTC+2 in March (EET); daylight saving time in summer
Climate: March is generally mild but can be rainy; bring a light jacket
